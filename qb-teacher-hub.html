Teachers may correct or withdraw their own Unverified/Returned submissions. A Returned question can be corrected and resubmitted for verification. My Revision History now shows the recorded return, correction, resubmission and verification trail without changing the question. Verified questions remain locked. While composing a new question, this device keeps a local safety copy. A local review helper also flags safe spacing/capitalisation clean-ups after typing or dictation; it never changes wording automatically.
