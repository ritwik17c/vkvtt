⚠ Correction warnings & practical tips
Do not invent dates. When the workbook total and dates disagree, verify the original Excel row, leave application/sanction, attendance, proxy record or another approved school source.

When resolving a parked item

• Use one row per actual date.
• Leave type/category can be different on every date.
• Full Leave normally counts 1.0; Half Leave normally counts 0.5.
• For half leave, select P1–P4 or P5–P8 as actually approved.
• The total leave units must reconcile with the parked legacy amount before the app will save it.

When editing an existing leave

• Past, present and future records may be edited here.
• A past edit does not silently rewrite an already-finalised proxy sheet; the app warns you if a published proxy exists for an affected date.
• Saving an existing multi-date record converts it to date-level records so each date can carry its own category/status.
• Every save creates an Admin audit entry.

⏳ Pending Leave Resolutions

These are the items temporarily parked with the book/accounting action during legacy import. They do not create proxies until resolved into genuine dated records.
