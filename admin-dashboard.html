📅 Edit Master Timetable

Select the exact slot. Only teachers free in that slot are offered as new choices. The teacher(s) already attached to the current entry remain visible even when the same slot is shared with another class.
Choose a slot.
Tick one or more teachers where the period is split between groups.

Need a teacher who is busy in this slot?

Choose that teacher and the app will look for another slot of the same class where the teacher is free. Where a safe two-way swap is possible, it is shown as a suggested swap.

👩‍🏫 Teachers & Workload

This panel reflects the current master timetable. Combined/shared entries in the same slot are counted once per teacher per day/period.

🏫 Classes & Sections

Current class/section structure and the number of master timetable entries for each.

📜 Master Timetable Change History

The dashboard keeps the most recent 100 master timetable changes inside the protected master document. Restoring a previous slot creates a new history entry rather than erasing the audit trail.
